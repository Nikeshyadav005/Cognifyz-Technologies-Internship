# Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship – Task Summary

The Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship consists of three levels, with interns required to complete any two. 
Each level contains a series of data analysis tasks designed to enhance analytical, visualization, and reporting skills.

Level 1 – Basic Data Insights

(1) Top Cuisines:

Identify the top three most common cuisines.

Calculate the percentage of restaurants serving each.

(2) City Analysis:

Find the city with the highest number of restaurants.

Compute average restaurant ratings per city and identify the highest-rated city.

(3) Price Range Distribution:

Create a histogram or bar chart for price ranges.

Calculate the percentage of restaurants per price range.

(4) Online Delivery:

Determine the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery.

Compare average ratings between restaurants with and without online delivery.


Level 2 – Intermediate Data Exploration

(1) Restaurant Ratings:

Analyze distribution of aggregate ratings.

Find the most common rating range and average number of votes.

(2) Cuisine Combination:

Identify common cuisine combinations.

Check if certain combinations have higher ratings.

(3) Geographic Analysis:

Plot restaurant locations on a map (longitude & latitude).

Identify clusters or geographic patterns.

(4) Restaurant Chains:

Detect restaurant chains in the dataset.

Analyze ratings and popularity of chains.


Level 3 – Advanced Analytical Insights

(1) Restaurant Reviews:

Analyze text reviews for common positive and negative keywords.

Examine the relationship between review length and ratings.

(2) Votes Analysis:

Identify restaurants with the most and fewest votes.

Explore correlation between votes and ratings.

(3) Price Range vs. Services:

Study the relationship between price range and availability of online delivery/table booking.

Assess if higher-priced restaurants offer more services.
